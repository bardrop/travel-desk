# Video script (about 1 min 50 s)

Rules from Devpost: under 2 minutes, show the project running in a browser, **you** explain it in your own voice (not AI), public on YouTube, no third-party music or trademarks.

Set up before you record:

- Open the live site in a clean browser window, at the top of the page.
- Have a second tab ready with `?demo=1` (offline demo).
- Have your AI key saved in **AI model settings** in the first tab (low spending limit). Do not show the key on screen.
- Close other tabs. Turn off notifications. No music.

Say it in your own words. The lines below are a guide.

| Time | On screen | You say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | The hero with the five agent boxes | "This is Travel Desk. It's an open agentic website. Five AI travel agents plan a trip together, right here in my browser." |
| 0:12–0:25 | Click `travel_desk.network.json` in the hero caption, scroll it briefly, go back | "The whole agent team is one public JSON file, following the open agentic web spec. A front desk, plus a logistics scout, a budget analyst, an itinerary planner and a local guide." |
| 0:25–0:35 | Scroll to the chat. Open AI model settings for one second (key hidden), close it | "It runs on my own AI key. The key stays in my browser. The site has no server and pays for nothing." |
| 0:35–1:05 | Type: "Plan the days of my current trip: one big sight each morning and something fun each evening." Scroll up so the agent map and the day board are visible while it works | "I ask for a plan. Watch the boxes: the front desk hands the job to the itinerary planner. The planner uses the page's own tools and fills the day board. Then the budget analyst prices it." |
| 1:05–1:20 | Point at the budget panel and the cap box | "The budget updates on the page. It says my trip is tight against my cap, and how much I save in budget style. These are planning estimates, not quotes." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Click the "Start a new trip" demo prompt in the demo tab, show the dialog, click "Start the new trip" | "Anything that can't be undone asks me first. Here the page shows exactly what will change before it runs." |
| 1:35–1:45 | Ask "Can you book our flights and pay for them?" | "And it's honest: it can't book or take payments, so it says so." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Scroll to "How it works" | "Other agentic websites can call this desk by its URL. No key? Try the offline demo. Thanks for watching." |

Tips:

- If the live model is slow, cut the waiting in editing. Do not speed up your voice.
- Say the short version if you run long: drop the 1:35 part first.
- Keep the video under 1:59.
