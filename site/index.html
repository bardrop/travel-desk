          How it works

          1. Public JSON

            The five agents, their instructions and their tools are one data file: travel_desk.network.json, listed in agent-manifest.json. No code, no secrets.

          2. Runs in your browser

            The open-source runtime in runtime/ reads that file and runs the agents in this tab. There is no agent server.

          3. Your key, your model

            You pick the AI provider and model. Your key stays in this browser and goes only to that provider. The site pays for nothing and sees nothing.

          4. Real tools, real limits

            The agents use this page's own functions as tools: read the trip, price it, fill the board. Starting over asks you first.

          5. Callable by other sites

            Other agentic websites can call this desk at travel_desk. They run it on their visitor's key and get advice, not access to this page.

          Travel Desk plans and estimates. It does not book, take payments or check visas. Amounts are rough 2026 planning bands, not quotes.
